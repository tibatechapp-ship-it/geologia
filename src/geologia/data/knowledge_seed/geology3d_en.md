# 3D geological modeling — evidence and uncertainty

Original GEOLOGIA checklist for preparing structural geology inputs. A 3D model is an interpretation constrained by observations, not a direct measurement of the subsurface.

## Inputs to inventory
- Geological map and map scale, legend, mapping date, and source.
- Stratigraphic units, contacts, observed/assumed contact type, and stratigraphic relationships.
- Structural measurements with location, azimuth convention, dip, measurement method, and quality.
- Fault traces, fault sense where observed, offsets, and uncertainty.
- Borehole constraints, lithology logs, geophysical sections, DEM, and the CRS/vertical datum of each source.

## Model preparation
1. Separate observed points/lines from interpreted or digitized features.
2. Check orientation conventions (dip direction versus strike/dip), units, duplicate measurements, and coordinate consistency.
3. Establish stratigraphic order and explicitly document unconformities or uncertain relationships.
4. Keep model extent within the support of the observations; mark extrapolated regions.
5. Preserve alternative interpretations where data do not uniquely constrain geometry.

## Review gate
Report input coverage, assumptions, model version, and uncertainty. Do not present a smooth surface or visually plausible model as proof of a geological structure, reserve, or drilling target.
