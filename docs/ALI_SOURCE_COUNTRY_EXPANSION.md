  - Lists Afghanistan, Indonesia and Malaysia among geoscience institution/partner contexts.

These directories are evidence for identifying organizations, not proof that every linked URL is current, that every institution provides an open repository, or that content may be downloaded or redistributed.

## Country-specific verification notes

- **Türkiye:** MTA is an established national mineral/geological research institution; its current English portal is the discovery seed.
- **Malaysia:** JMG is the national minerals and geoscience department; the official FAQ describes its geoscience data and research functions.
- **Indonesia:** Geological Agency under the Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources; its official portal is the seed.
- **Venezuela:** The catalog currently uses a ministry-level discovery portal. The specific current geological survey/research unit and repository endpoint remain to be verified; do not treat this seed as a validated geological data endpoint.
- **Pakistan:** Geological Survey of Pakistan is identified in international geological-survey directories. The catalog URL and repository endpoints remain marked for current-domain verification.
- **Afghanistan:** The Ministry of Mines and Petroleum portal is a ministry-level seed. The specific survey archive and its access status must be located and verified.
- **Algeria:** ASGA is identified in GSJ/AIST's international directory; verify the current official HTTPS domain and repository before crawling.
- **Morocco:** IUGS identifies the Direction de la Géologie under the ministry. The current department structure, portal and repository need confirmation.
- **Mauritania:** The catalog is a ministry-level discovery seed; verify the current geology directorate, official domain and data repository.
- **Sudan:** GRAS is a candidate geological research authority; current official domain, operational status and data access require verification.

## Acquisition controls

No crawler should be enabled from this catalog alone. Before acquisition, verify:

1. official domain and responsible institution;
2. repository/API endpoint and technical documentation;
3. robots policy, terms and rate limits;
4. per-resource license and redistribution conditions;
5. source attribution, versioning and checksum strategy.

Keep original-language records (Arabic, French, Turkish, Malay, Indonesian, Spanish, Urdu, Dari/Pashto and English where applicable). Translation must remain a linked derivative, never a replacement for the original source.
