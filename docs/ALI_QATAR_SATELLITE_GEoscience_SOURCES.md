# Qatar — Satellite, Earth Observation and Geoscience Research Sources

**Status:** source discovery seeds; not harvested, licensed, or ingested into Ali's knowledge store.

## Research and geoscience

- **Qatar University Earth Sciences Cluster** — research on terrestrial/geological systems, coastal and built environments, marine/atmospheric interfaces, and geospatial analysis. Official page: https://qu.edu.qa/en-us/Research/esc/Research/Pages/earth-sciences.aspx
- **Qatar University Remote Sensing and GIS Laboratory** — describes satellite and aerial imagery archives and analysis for environmental monitoring, coastal/marine science, soils, geomorphology, geological formations, sabkhas, erosion and sediment distribution. Official page: https://engineering.qu.edu.qa/en-us/research/esc/facilities/labs/remote-sensing-and-gis
- **QUbeSat1** — Qatar University nanosatellite/CubeSat research project. University publication reports the project and support from QRDI and Es'hailSat. Reference: https://www.qu.edu.qa/en-us/about/Documents/Campus%20Life%20%28E%29%20-%20May%202026.pdf
- **Space Radio Monitoring Center (SRMC)** — Qatar News Agency reports satellite communication monitoring and spectrum/interference-related capabilities. A dedicated official research/publication portal remains to be identified. Reference: https://qna.org.qa/en/news/news-bulletins-details?date=10%2F10%2F2024&id=0056-minister-of-communication-to-qna%C2%A0srmc-enhances-qatar%27s%C2%A0leadership-in-space-communications

## Satellite companies and commercial capabilities

- **Es'hailSat (Qatar Satellite Company)** — national satellite operator; its official site lists Es'hail-1 and Es'hail-2 as communications satellites supporting broadcasting, mobility and telecommunications. Do not label these spacecraft as Earth-observation imaging satellites. Official site: https://www.eshailsat.qa/
- **Bayanat Engineering Qatar — Earth Observation & Remote Sensing Division** — company announcement describes satellite imagery and geospatial analytics services. For each product, verify the source constellation, sensor, spatial resolution, revisit, coverage, license, and permitted use before considering imagery for GEOLOGIA. Reference: https://www.bayanatengineering.qa/bayanat-engineering-qatar-launches-earth-observation-remote-sensing-division/

## Emerging partnerships

Es'hailSat's 2026 timeline describes agreements involving Türksat and Thales Alenia Space for the Es'hail-3/Türksat-Biruni satellite project and a Telesat partnership framework for exploring LEO connectivity. These are relevant to satellite communications and industrial capabilities, but should not be treated as Earth-observation data sources without a confirmed imaging payload and data policy.

Official timeline: https://www.eshailsat.qa/timeline/

## Ali ingestion checklist

For every Qatar source, Ali's source-discovery workflow should collect and validate:

1. institution/company legal identity and official domain;
2. technical publications, mission descriptions and public datasets;
3. satellite name, mission type, orbit, payload/sensor and operating status;
4. imagery source, acquisition date, resolution, coverage and processing level;
5. DOI, stable URL, dataset identifier and source version;
6. license, access conditions, attribution, and redistribution restrictions;
7. whether the resource is geoscience/Earth observation or communications-only.

No scraping or bulk downloading should begin until access and license checks are complete. Keep satellite communications records separate from Earth-observation data and from geological research evidence.
